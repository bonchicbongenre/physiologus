What asciimoose.mov does. A golf pass must keep every line true.

 1. One film at a time, on its own black ground: 107 films. The room is as wide as
    lets the film, its label and the way on fit one screen without scrolling.
 2. Under the film, its label: the title; its medium in small caps (single-channel
    video, colour or black and white, silent or sound); 2026; its running time, in
    lining tabular figures. A screen reader hears the title when the film changes.
 3. "next random" deals a film not yet shown this visit, never the one on screen;
    when every film has been shown it deals the room again. The right arrow key
    does the same. "N of 107" gives the film's number in the catalogue.
 4. "previous", and the left arrow key, walk back through what was watched;
    "previous" appears only when there is somewhere to go back to.
 5. Every film has its own address, #its-title, and the back button walks the same
    trail. A bare address shows the first film, an ascii moose.
 6. A silent film plays at once, muted, on a loop; clicking it pauses and resumes.
    A film with sound waits, with its controls. With reduced motion every film
    waits, with its controls, showing its first frame.
 7. "catalogue" folds out the room's own label, with the page's size read live, and
    every title in catalogue order with its running time. Each title links to its
    file; with script, it shows that film in the room and brings the room into
    view. The film showing is marked.
 8. The console gives the Greek word for the one who reads nature, and what it
    means.
 9. Colours come from the house tokens, crimson; dark mode follows the system.
    The films' ground is their own black.
10. The 404 is the label of a film that has not been made.
11. The favicon is Fred's own outline, in crimson, lighter on dark tab bars.
